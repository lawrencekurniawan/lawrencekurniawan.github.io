Keepsake

Personal flashcards for words, phrases, and grammar patterns.

Keepsake is a personal iOS flashcard app. A topic groups the words, phrases, or grammar patterns you are learning. Cards are forward-only: the term is on the front; meaning, usage, a mnemonic, and examples are on the back.

What it does

  • Schedules reviews with FSRS-6, a spaced-repetition algorithm.
  • Imports cards from CSV files or a Google Sheet you choose, with duplicate rows grouped before import.
  • Can fill missing meaning, mnemonics, and examples through OpenAI, using your own API key.
  • Stores topics, cards, review history, and settings locally on your device.
  • Exports and restores a versioned, checksummed backup file that you control.

How it is distributed

Keepsake is a personal project, not an App Store app. It is built as an unsigned iOS package and installed with SideStore inside LiveContainer using a free Apple ID. There is no account, subscription, analytics service, or backend server.

Network access is limited to the services you enable: OpenAI for AI-assisted content, and Google for OAuth and Sheets when you connect a spreadsheet. The app does not contact any other host.
